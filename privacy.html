English

We do not collect any personal data.

Kanso needs no account or sign-in. It contains no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking code, makes no network requests, and sends nothing to our servers.

Camera and photos: the camera is used only to photograph your own items. Choosing from Photos uses the system picker, so the app receives only the photo you pick. Cutouts are made on your device.

Stored on your device: item images, their positions, what is stored where, and interface settings. This stays on your device and is removed, permanently, when you delete the app.

Sharing: the share image is created on your device. It leaves it only when you tap share and pick a destination in the system share sheet.

Changes: if cloud sync or other features that process data are added later, this page and the App Store privacy details will be updated first.
