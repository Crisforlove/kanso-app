怎么用

添加物品：点底部的加号，选“拍摄”或“图库”，确认抠图后物品会出现在画布上。

摆放：单指拖动物品；长按可以调整大小或删除。双指平移和缩放画布，双击空白处回到全部物品。

收纳：把包或盒子拖到顶部，它就成了收纳空间。把别的物品拖到它上面就收进去；点它查看里面，把物品拖出来即可取出。把它向下拖回画布，会变回普通物品并放出里面的东西。

常备：长按架上的包，选“存为常备”。之后从屏幕侧边的环里把它拉出来拖到顶部，里面的东西会一起归位。

数据：全部保存在本机，没有云备份。删除 App 后无法找回。

How it works

Add an item: tap + at the bottom, choose Camera or Photos, and confirm the cutout. The item appears on the canvas.

Arrange: drag an item with one finger; touch and hold to resize or delete. Use two fingers to pan and zoom, and double-tap empty space to see everything.

Store: drag a bag or box to the top and it becomes storage. Drag other items onto it to put them away; tap it to look inside and drag items out. Drag it back down to the canvas to turn it back into a single item and release its contents.

Kits: touch and hold a bag on the shelf and choose Save as Kit. Later, pull it out of the ring at the side of the screen and drag it to the top; its contents come back with it.

Data: everything is stored on your device with no cloud backup. It cannot be recovered after the app is deleted.
